true graded detection correctly — the `(国際)` mapping is redundant and wrong.

Phase-6 reconciliation note; `tests/scraper/test_parser.py` updated to lock in
the fix and prevent regression. This plan does NOT touch
`src/pipeline/column_mapping.py` (Kaggle-side, out of scope — flagged for
Phase 6).
